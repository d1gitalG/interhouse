# InterHouse Roblox Roadmap Council Pass

_Date: 2026-05-16_
_Status: VALID_COUNCIL, roadmap artifact_

## Decision Question

Given the deeper Roblox concept, where should InterHouse Sports Day start, and how should it progress without overbuilding?

## Source Context

- Root fantasy: Nigerian-style inter-house school sports.
- Player identity layers: student, House, school, cohort/alumni.
- Core mechanics: practice, specialization, hidden aptitude, trials, Sports Day, defending champions, graduation.
- Expansion mechanics: multiple school types, intra-school competition, inter-school competition.
- Constraint: no pay-to-win, no wagering, no Robux prizes, no crypto, no paid stat advantage.

## Council Result

### Pena

Pick: **One-school prototype first**

Why: The bottleneck is proving that training from weak to useful feels good. Multi-school and graduation are expansion, not the first build.

Risk: Starting with five campuses kills speed.

Next action: Build one campus, four Houses, three events, one scoreboard.

### Kiyosaki

Pick: **Admission/style monetization later**

Why: The asset is retention and identity. Monetization should follow proof of repeated play, not precede it.

Risk: Paid admission too early looks like cash grab or pay-to-win even if stats are fair.

Next action: Keep v1 free/open. Design paid private-school access only after the core loop works.

### Naval

Pick: **Training system as the compounding engine**

Why: The hidden profile, alumni, records, and cohorts create long-term compounding identity.

Risk: Making hidden stats too complex before players understand the game.

Next action: Start with 3 hidden fields only: discipline, event aptitude, pressure.

### Hormozi

Pick: **Simple promise: become your House's specialist**

Why: The value prop is sharper than “play sports minigames.” The player wants to become needed by their House.

Risk: If everyone can do everything, no one has status.

Next action: Force soft specialization by limiting high-level training energy per term.

### Jobs

Pick: **Make the school feel alive before the system gets huge**

Why: The first campus must be memorable: uniforms, fields, banners, assembly, trophy hall, chants.

Risk: A spreadsheet with minigames will feel dead.

Next action: Put ceremony and House identity in v1, even if the sports list is small.

### Bezos

Pick: **Trust and fairness from day one**

Why: Hidden mechanics can feel rigged unless the player gets understandable feedback.

Risk: Hidden aptitude becomes frustration if players cannot tell why they are improving or failing.

Next action: Use coach hints and visible progress bands, not full stat sheets.

### Drucker

Pick: **Measure term completion**

Why: The project lives or dies on whether players finish a training cycle and return for the event.

Risk: Optimizing for session minigame plays instead of season commitment.

Next action: Track first practice complete, second practice return, trial qualification, Sports Day attendance, re-enrollment after graduation.

### Analyst

Pick: **Stage gates**

Why: This concept can sprawl. Each phase needs a proof gate before the next layer.

Risk: Graduation and inter-school systems are expensive if the base game is not sticky.

Next action: Phase roadmap with clear gates.

## Recommendation

Start with **InterHouse Sports Day: First School Prototype**.

Do not start with multi-school, full graduation, inter-school tournaments, or deep monetization.

Start with one school that proves:

1. Players understand House identity.
2. Players enjoy starting weak and training up.
3. Players naturally specialize.
4. House scoreboard movement feels meaningful.
5. Sports Day ceremony feels worth showing up for.

## Phase Roadmap

### Phase 0: Paper Prototype / Design Lock

Goal: define the smallest version before building.

Scope:

- 4 Houses
- 1 school campus
- 3 event lanes: Sprint, Long Jump, Sigil Clash
- 1 semester cycle
- 1 Sports Day ceremony
- 3 hidden stats only: discipline, event aptitude, pressure

Done when:

- one-page game loop exists
- event mechanics are defined
- progression math is rough but coherent
- no-pay-to-win economy rules are written

### Phase 1: Roblox Core Loop Prototype

Goal: make one player train and feel improvement.

Scope:

- campus spawn
- House assignment
- sprint drill
- long-jump drill
- Sigil Clash drill
- visible beginner weakness
- basic event mastery
- House XP gain
- coach hint after practice

Do not build yet:

- graduation
- multiple schools
- paid admission
- inter-school competition
- complex hidden personality systems

Proof gate:

- player can complete 10 minutes of training and understand what improved.

### Phase 2: House Competition Prototype

Goal: make House identity matter.

Scope:

- House scoreboard
- practice meets
- trial qualification
- simple House roles: runner, jumper, tactician, spirit/support
- top contributors board
- House banners/colors/chant moments

Proof gate:

- players care whether their House is winning.
- at least 2 event lanes have different specialists.

### Phase 3: First Sports Day Event

Goal: make the main event feel meaningful.

Scope:

- scheduled Sports Day event
- opening ceremony
- finals for three events
- live scoreboard
- champion House reveal
- trophy hall update
- defending champion banner

Proof gate:

- players return for the event instead of only grinding practice.

### Phase 4: Student Lifecycle

Goal: add seasons, cohorts, graduation, and legacy.

Scope:

- Year 1 / Year 2 / Year 3 framing
- graduation after 3-4 Sports Days
- alumni records
- retired student profile
- new student enrollment
- legacy cosmetics or alumni wall

Proof gate:

- players accept graduation because legacy feels valuable, not like deletion.

### Phase 5: Multi-School Expansion

Goal: add world scale after the first school works.

Scope:

- public/open school
- private school variant
- scholarship academy
- school-specific uniforms/campuses/traditions
- school enrollment gates
- no paid stat advantages

Proof gate:

- schools feel meaningfully different without becoming pay-to-win.

### Phase 6: Inter-School Competition

Goal: expand from House pride to school pride.

Scope:

- school teams
- inter-school qualifiers
- regional Sports Day
- school rivalries
- regional champion school

Proof gate:

- players identify with both House and school.

## MVP Build Slice

The first playable Roblox slice should be:

- One school
- Four Houses
- House assignment
- Sprint practice
- Long Jump practice
- Sigil Clash practice
- House XP scoreboard
- coach hints
- one small practice meet

That is enough to prove whether the core has legs.

## Progression Principle

Use **soft locks**, not hard frustration.

Players can try everything, but championship-level growth requires focus:

- training energy per term limits elite progress
- event mastery rises fastest in chosen path
- coach recommendations nudge specialization
- trials require minimum event mastery
- all players can still earn House points through practice and support

## Hidden Mechanics Boundary

Hidden systems should guide identity, not confuse players.

Start with only:

- discipline: consistency and practice habits
- aptitude: event-specific growth tendency
- pressure: finals/trial performance modifier

Show hints, not raw numbers.

Examples:

- Coach: `You're becoming reliable in relay drills.`
- Captain: `We may need you for the 100m trials.`
- Report: `Strong practice form, but pressure still shaky.`

## Monetization Timing

Do not monetize until Phase 2 or later.

First monetization candidates:

- uniforms
- dorm cosmetics
- House banner cosmetics
- extra student save slot
- private server school cohort
- ceremony effects

Never sell:

- better aptitude
- faster training
- guaranteed trial slots
- captain status
- Sports Day qualification
