# InterHouse Roblox Big Vision Addendum

_Date: 2026-05-16_
_Status: VALID_COUNCIL addendum / scale correction_

## Trigger

Gianni challenged the prior roadmap as too small and reframed the opportunity:

- Roblox has massive player scale.
- The build process itself should be marketing.
- The game should aim to become a rite of passage for Roblox kids as they age through the platform.
- AI/LLMs can let the system become deeper than human-timeline thinking allows.
- The roadmap should not shrink the vision just because the first build must be playable.

## Roblox Scale Check

Recent reported Roblox Q3 2025 metrics:

- ~151.5 million daily active users.
- ~39.6 billion hours engaged in Q3 2025.
- Users aged 13+ reportedly made up about two-thirds of DAUs and grew strongly year over year.

Implication: the ambition is justified. Roblox is large enough for a culturally specific, long-horizon social game to matter if the experience is sticky and legible.

## Council Correction

The previous council roadmap was right about build order but too conservative in framing.

Corrected stance:

- **Vision should be huge.**
- **First playable slice should still be focused.**
- The prototype should not feel like a tiny minigame. It should feel like the first campus of a much larger school universe.

## Big Vision

InterHouse should aim to become a Roblox rite-of-passage game:

> Enroll as a student. Join a House. Train through school terms. Represent your House. Graduate into legacy. Watch the next cohort rise.

The long-term fantasy is not only winning minigames. It is growing up inside a school world.

## Build-In-Public Marketing Strategy

The marketing is the making-of.

Content themes:

- why students start weak
- why hidden aptitude exists
- how Houses create identity
- why graduation matters
- how no-pay-to-win admission works
- why public school vs private school matters
- how inter-house becomes inter-school
- how AI makes coaches/captains feel alive
- why this comes from Nigerian inter-house sports

Share the reasoning without exposing every hidden mechanic.

Examples:

- show a runner improving across weeks
- show a House losing because it ignored field events
- show a first-year becoming a relay anchor
- show a senior graduating into the Hall of Fame
- show public school students beating an elite campus
- show coach/captain AI giving personality-safe feedback

## Human-Timeline vs Machine-Timeline Split

Do not think small because humans build slowly.

But also do not build chaos.

Use parallel tracks:

1. **Playable Core Track**
   - one campus
   - four Houses
   - three events
   - training progression
   - House scoreboard

2. **Lore / World Track**
   - school types
   - House traditions
   - graduation ceremonies
   - inter-school ladder
   - cohort history

3. **AI Systems Track**
   - coach hints
   - captain personalities
   - safe student report cards
   - event recommendations
   - rivalry summaries

4. **Content / Marketing Track**
   - devlog clips
   - system explainers
   - test seasons
   - community polls
   - behind-the-build storytelling

The public sees the world expanding while the playable build stays disciplined.

## AI/LLM Role

Use AI where it adds living-school depth, not where it risks moderation or chaos.

Good AI uses:

- coach feedback generated from safe templates
- House captain summaries
- student report cards
- alumni blurbs
- rivalry recaps
- ceremony announcements
- practice recommendations
- behind-the-scenes balancing analysis

Risky AI uses:

- open freeform chat with kids
- unmoderated player-generated prompts
- unpredictable trash talk
- gameplay-critical black-box decisions

Principle:

> AI should make the school feel alive. It should not decide winners in a way players cannot trust.

## Revised Starting Point

Start with the same first campus, but build it as **Campus 001** of a bigger universe.

The first prototype should include visible hints of depth:

- locked trophy hall wings
- future school map board
- alumni wall placeholder
- term calendar
- coming inter-school notice board
- coach office
- House history plaques

This lets marketing tell the big story without requiring every system to be built on day one.

## Updated Recommendation

Go as deep as Gianni wants in the **world model, content strategy, and system architecture**.

Stay narrow in the **first playable implementation**.

The right move is not small vision. It is:

> Big mythology, focused first campus.

## Next Action

Create a Phase 0 build spec that contains two layers:

1. **Playable v0.1**: what builders implement first.
2. **Visible Future Hooks**: what players/viewers see that proves the world is bigger.
