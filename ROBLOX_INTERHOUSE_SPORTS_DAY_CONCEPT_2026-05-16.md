# InterHouse Roblox Deep Concept - Nigerian School Sports Model

_Date: 2026-05-16_
_Status: concept expansion from Gianni voice note_

## Core Insight

InterHouse should not be treated as a generic Roblox battle arena. The strongest version comes from the Nigerian school inter-house sports model:

- The school is divided into Houses.
- Students practice through the term/semester.
- Practice builds pride, readiness, and points.
- The main Inter-House Sports event is the big social climax.
- One House becomes champion for the semester.
- Next semester, that House returns as defending champion.
- Rivalries and house identity carry across seasons.

That model is already Roblox-native: teams, training, minigames, cosmetics, leaderboards, ceremonies, seasonal resets, and social competition.

## Revised Game Shape

Working title: **InterHouse: Sports Day** or **InterHouse: Academy Trials**

Short pitch:

> Join a House, train all term, compete in school events, and help your House win Sports Day.

This is stronger than making the whole game only about AI duels. Duels can still exist, but they become one event category inside a wider inter-house season.

## Season Structure

### 1. House Assignment

Players join one of four Houses. Each House has:

- color
- mascot/sigil
- chant
- banner
- dorm/training area
- current rank
- historical trophies
- defending champion status if applicable

### 2. Practice Phase

Most normal gameplay happens during practice week/semester. Players earn House XP through events like:

- sprint training
- relay drills
- long jump
- obstacle course
- javelin/throwing minigame
- tug-of-war
- reaction drills
- strategy duels / Sigil Clash
- rhythm/chant practice
- mascot errands or school tasks

Practice should feel useful, not fake. It should improve:

- personal mastery
- event-specific stats
- cosmetic unlocks
- House morale
- qualification for finals
- team relay roles

Players should start weak. A new runner should feel slow, a new jumper should mistime jumps, and a new thrower should have poor control. The fantasy is not instant competence. The fantasy is training across a semester until your House can trust you in the main event.

### Training Paths / Specialization

Players should not be able to become elite at every event at once. They need to choose paths.

Example paths:

- **Sprinter**: acceleration, top speed, stamina, lane control
- **Relay Runner**: sprint speed, baton timing, team chemistry
- **Jumper**: approach rhythm, takeoff timing, air control, landing
- **Thrower**: strength, aim, release timing, technique
- **Tactician**: Sigil Clash reads, counter-picks, composure, bluff discipline
- **House Spirit**: chant timing, morale boosts, banner work, crowd energy

This creates real roles inside each House:

- one player becomes the relay anchor
- one becomes the long-jump specialist
- one becomes the Sigil Clash captain
- one becomes a morale/support player
- one becomes the all-around but not best-in-class competitor

The game should reward specialization without making casual players useless. A player can dabble everywhere, but championship-level slots require focused training.

### Hidden Aptitude / Specialist Identity

Training should affect more than visible XP. The game should quietly build a hidden student profile based on what the player actually practices.

Example hidden factors:

- event aptitude
- consistency
- pressure rating
- discipline
- stamina ceiling
- team chemistry
- leadership potential
- clutch factor
- injury/fatigue risk, if used lightly

If a player only trains sprinting, the system should start treating them like a runner. That may unlock better sprint potential, relay roles, speed-based cosmetics, or coach recommendations. But it may also make them weaker in throwing, jumping, or tactics.

This should be mostly hidden because school sports should feel like identity forming over time, not a spreadsheet. Players see hints through coaches, results, titles, and opportunities:

- "Coach says you are becoming a relay specialist."
- "Your House captain wants you in the 100m trials."
- "You lack field-event reps."
- "You are reliable under finals pressure."

The player should not see every stat. They should feel discovered.

### Early Difficulty

The first sessions should feel a little hard on purpose:

- sprint speed starts low
- jumps are awkward until timing improves
- throws wobble until technique improves
- Sigil Clash reads are basic until the player trains tactics
- stamina limits prevent endless event grinding

This gives practice meaning. Players should feel themselves getting better over days/weeks, not just collecting numbers.

Suggested progression:

1. **Novice**: learning controls, slow stats, easy mistakes
2. **House Regular**: useful in practice meets
3. **Specialist**: can qualify for House trials
4. **Representative**: chosen for Sports Day finals
5. **Champion / Record Holder**: remembered in House history

### Student Lifecycle / Graduation

Students should not stay forever. The school should work in cohorts.

Each player-created student has a school career:

- Year 1: raw new student
- Year 2: developing specialist
- Year 3: senior / captain candidate
- Graduation: student retires into school history

Graduation solves several problems:

- prevents one maxed student from dominating forever
- creates room for new students
- makes each Sports Day season meaningful
- gives old students legacy value
- makes enrollment feel important

Graduated students can become:

- Hall of Fame records
- House alumni portraits
- coach NPCs
- legacy cosmetics
- retired captains
- inspiration bonuses for future students in the same House

The player does not lose everything. They lose the active student, but keep account-level prestige, cosmetics, alumni history, and maybe a small legacy effect.

Best default for Roblox:

- Do not use real-time years.
- Use seasons/terms.
- Example: one student career lasts 3-4 Sports Day seasons.
- After graduation, the player enrolls a new student.

### Private School / Enrollment Gate

The school should feel selective. Not everybody just spawns as a champion.

Enrollment can be the framing for new character creation:

- choose or receive House placement
- pass a short entrance trial
- get evaluated by coaches
- receive an initial aptitude hint
- start as a weak first-year student

Possible enrollment mechanics:

- free base enrollment slot
- limited active student slots
- new cohort openings each season
- private-server school cohorts for friend groups
- cosmetic-only premium uniforms or dorm items

Avoid pay-to-win admissions. Paid access should not create better athletes. It can create more style, more save slots, or private-school server tools.

### Multi-School World

Admission can become worldbuilding instead of power.

The first school should be the default campus, but the game can expand into multiple schools with different identities:

- public school
- standard private school
- elite private school
- scholarship academy
- old legacy school
- sports-focused academy
- rough underdog school

Each school can have:

- different visual quality
- different campus layout
- different enrollment capacity
- different House traditions
- different uniforms
- different prestige level
- different event calendar
- different rival schools
- different admission friction

Important rule: better-looking or harder-to-enter schools should not create stronger athletes by default. The player still has to train. Prestige affects framing, cosmetics, social status, campus access, school culture, and competition tier, not raw pay-to-win power.

Example school tiers:

1. **Open Public School**
   - admits many students
   - cheaper/common uniforms
   - crowded practice fields
   - chaotic but high-energy
   - great for onboarding and mass events

2. **Standard Private School**
   - cleaner campus
   - better facilities visually
   - more organized Houses
   - moderate admission friction
   - good default fantasy

3. **Elite Legacy School**
   - expensive-looking campus
   - limited cohort slots
   - strong traditions
   - trophy halls, prefects, ceremonies
   - admission feels selective

4. **Scholarship Academy**
   - admits talented students through trials
   - underdog-to-elite story
   - prestige comes from performance, not money

This gives the game long-term expansion:

- start at one school
- graduate students
- unlock/apply to new schools
- run inter-school tournaments
- create school rivalries
- let private servers host their own school cohorts

### Intra-School vs Inter-School Competition

The competitive structure should have two layers:

1. **Intra-school Inter-House Sports**
   - Houses inside one school battle each other.
   - Red/Blue/Green/Yellow or equivalent Houses compete across practice, trials, and Sports Day.
   - One House becomes the school champion for that term.
   - This is the main early-game loop.

2. **Inter-school Sports**
   - Schools battle other schools.
   - A school's champion House or selected school team represents the campus.
   - This creates bigger rivalries: school pride on top of House pride.
   - This becomes the mid/late-game expansion layer.

Example ladder:

- Practice drills
- House trials
- School Sports Day
- School champion crowned
- Inter-school qualifier
- Inter-school Sports Day
- Regional champion school crowned

This gives players multiple identities:

- their student
- their House
- their school
- their alumni/cohort

It also lets the game scale without losing the original Nigerian school-sports inspiration. First you fight for your House inside school. Then your school fights the outside world.

### Admission Monetization Boundary

If players pay for admission, what they are paying for should be access/style/social experience, not guaranteed wins.

Allowed:

- school access pass
- private school server
- extra student save slot
- premium uniform cosmetics
- dorm/campus cosmetics
- alumni wall cosmetics
- ceremony effects

Avoid:

- paid stat boosts
- paid better aptitude
- paid guaranteed House captain slot
- paid Sports Day qualification
- paid hidden potential advantage

The clean principle:

> You can pay to attend a prettier or more exclusive school. You still have to earn your place on the track.

### 3. Trials / Qualification

Before the main event, each House runs internal trials. This creates status and repeat play:

- fastest sprinters qualify for relay
- best jumpers represent long jump
- top duelists represent Sigil Clash
- most active players become captains
- support players can still contribute through morale, banners, practice boosts, and crowd energy

This keeps casual players useful without letting a few sweats own the whole season.

### 4. Main Event: Inter-House Sports Day

This is the weekly or seasonal climax. It should feel like an actual school sports day:

- opening ceremony
- House parade
- chants
- event schedule
- scoreboard visible to everyone
- event finals
- surprise bonus event
- closing ceremony
- champion announcement
- trophy lift
- defending champion tag for next season

Roblox players need spectacle here. This is where the game can feel bigger than a minigame lobby.

### 5. Off-Season / Next Semester

After Sports Day:

- winning House gets trophy in hall
- players receive titles/cosmetics
- season stats archive
- defending champion banner appears next season
- rival Houses get comeback missions
- new event rotation begins

## Core Loop

Daily loop:

1. Enter school campus.
2. Check House board.
3. Choose a training path for the session.
4. Practice 2-3 quick drills in that path.
5. Earn event mastery, personal XP, and House points.
6. Unlock cosmetics or qualify for an event.
7. See House rank move.

Weekly/seasonal loop:

1. Practice period opens.
2. Players specialize and improve across the semester.
3. Houses run trials for event qualification spots.
4. Main Sports Day runs.
5. Champion House is crowned.
6. Defending champion status carries into next season.

## Where Original InterHouse Fits

The existing InterHouse agent/duel DNA should become **one event lane**, not the whole Roblox game.

Best fit:

- **Sigil Clash**: strategy duel event
- **House Captains**: AI-flavored champion NPCs that represent Houses
- **Coach Advice**: safe templated reasoning after matches
- **Rival Reads**: lightweight, prewritten strategy snippets

Example:

- Red House captain predicts Blue House is overusing Spark.
- Player chooses Veil in Sigil Clash.
- Result screen says: `Read: Blue leaned Spark. Plan: Veil counter. House morale +12.`

That preserves the soul of agent strategy without making Roblox depend on uncontrolled AI text.

## Event Ideas

### Track

- 100m sprint
- relay race
- hurdle dash
- stamina race

Mechanics:

- timing-based starts
- lane discipline
- boost zones
- baton handoff minigame

### Field

- long jump
- high jump
- javelin-style throw
- shot-put-style throw

Mechanics:

- charge meter
- angle selection
- timing release
- wind/random-lite environmental variation, not paid randomness

### House Spirit

- chant timing
- banner painting
- mascot run
- crowd rhythm game

Purpose:

Let non-competitive players contribute to House score.

### Strategy

- Sigil Clash
- captain duel
- prediction challenge

Purpose:

Carry over InterHouse's mind-game identity.

## Roblox-Safe Economy

Rewards should be prestige and cosmetics only:

- House uniforms
- event medals
- champion jackets
- trails
- emotes
- banners
- dorm decorations
- trophy room cosmetics
- captain aura effects

Do not include:

- Robux prizes
- betting
- staking
- paid random stat rolls
- crypto
- cash-out
- prize pools
- gambling-style wheels/chests

Monetization can be:

- cosmetic battle pass
- extra outfit slots
- private school server
- premium training room cosmetics
- house banner packs
- emote/chant packs
- announcer voice pack, if Roblox-safe

## MVP Recommendation

Build the smallest playable version as **Sports Day Prototype 001**:

1. Four Houses with colors and banners.
2. Campus lobby with scoreboard.
3. Three practice events:
   - sprint
   - long jump
   - Sigil Clash
4. House XP from each event.
5. Frequent **practice meets** every 30-60 minutes in-server.
6. A real **Sports Day championship** on a slower cadence, such as weekly or seasonal.
7. Champion House ceremony and defending champion banner until the next real championship.

Do not start with ten sports. Start with three great loops and ceremony.

## Cadence Correction

Sports Day should not create a new true champion every 30-60 minutes. That would make the title feel cheap.

Use two layers instead:

1. **Practice meets / mini-meets**
   - Runs every 30-60 minutes.
   - Gives players something live to do often.
   - Awards House XP, medals, temporary boosts, and qualification points.
   - Can name a mini-meet winner, but not the semester champion.

2. **True Sports Day championship**
   - Runs weekly, biweekly, monthly, or as a Roblox season.
   - Crowns the real champion House.
   - Creates the defending champion banner for the next season.
   - Should feel like the big school event, not a routine minigame.

Best default:

- Every 30-60 minutes: practice meet / heats / qualifiers.
- Every weekend or season: real Sports Day.
- Every season: defending champion history updates.

This preserves the Nigerian school model: lots of practice and buildup, then one meaningful main event.

## Why This Is Stronger

- It is culturally specific instead of generic.
- It naturally supports seasons and defending champions.
- It gives every player a reason to care even if they are not the best duelist.
- It works on Roblox without real-money mechanics.
- It gives InterHouse a visible world: school, Houses, practice grounds, sports day, trophy hall.
- It can still absorb the AI/agent strategy layer later as captains, coaches, and Sigil Clash.

## Best Product Direction

Lead with the school-sports fantasy:

> Pick your House. Train for Sports Day. Bring the trophy home.

Then use InterHouse's deeper strategy systems as the special sauce underneath, not as the first thing players have to understand.
