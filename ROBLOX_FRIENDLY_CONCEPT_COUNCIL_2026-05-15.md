# InterHouse Roblox-Friendly Concept Council

Date: 2026-05-15
Status: concept packet
Council status: VALID_COUNCIL

## Decision

Find Roblox-friendly versions of InterHouse that preserve the core magic:

- agents / houses with identity
- fast readable battles
- tournaments and spectator drama
- progression without gambling, real-money stakes, or adult framing
- UGC/social loops that fit Roblox

## Council Synthesis

Best direction: **House Trials Arena**.

Roblox InterHouse should not start as AI gambling, credits, or real-money tournaments. It should start as a social battle arena where players train, customize, and enter silly-but-skillful house champions into short automated duels and team events.

The product should feel like:

- tournament anime clubhouse
- pet/companion battler
- Roblox obby/minigame hub
- school house competition
- collectible personality engine

## Top Concepts

### 1. House Trials Arena

Players join a House, build a Champion, and queue into fast public trials: RPS duels, reaction minigames, prediction duels, maze races, dodge rounds, or arena hazards.

Why it works:
- keeps InterHouse identity and tournaments
- lets players understand outcomes visually
- avoids gambling/stakes
- Roblox-native: houses, hub, leaderboards, badges, cosmetics

Core loop:
1. Join a House.
2. Train Champion traits through minigames.
3. Enter Trial Brackets.
4. Win house points, badges, cosmetics, arena effects.
5. Spectate finals in a public arena.

### 2. Mascot Champions

Every player has a house mascot/champion that battles automatically based on personality traits, training choices, and loadout.

Examples:
- Ember Jackal-style aggressive champion
- Clockwork Swan-style disciplined champion
- Wicker Judge-style trap/setup champion

Roblox-friendly framing:
- no “agents wagering”
- no “AI gambling”
- instead: “your champion learned from your training”

### 3. Spectator Bracket Festivals

Timed server events run every 10 to 20 minutes. Players gather in stands while the top champions fight in a visible bracket.

Features:
- giant bracket wall
- house cheering zones
- emotes/confetti/cosmetics
- MVP replay boards
- “upset watch” and “finals path” cards

This translates InterHouse’s strongest existing asset: tournament spectacle.

### 4. House Missions

Players earn House Points through cooperative missions, not just duels.

Modes:
- capture relics
- defend the house banner
- solve puzzle rooms
- escort mascot
- team obby race

This gives younger players something active to do between automated battles.

### 5. Training Labs

Instead of prompting agents with private system prompts, players tune simple visible traits:

- Brave / Careful
- Tricky / Honest
- Fast / Patient
- Wild / Disciplined

Training minigames modify trait cards. This keeps the “agent personality” idea but makes it safe, visual, and kid-readable.

## Council Lens Notes

- Peña lens: start with one playable loop, not a giant metaverse. Build House Trials Arena as the wedge.
- Kiyosaki lens: monetize cosmetics, VIP house rooms, battle effects, emotes, and season passes. Avoid stakes.
- Naval lens: UGC + tournaments + shareable replays is the compounding loop.
- Hormozi lens: the offer is “build your champion, rep your house, win the arena.”
- Jobs lens: make it instantly legible. The player should know their House, Champion, next Trial, and reward within 10 seconds.
- Bezos lens: recurring events create the flywheel: train -> compete -> spectate -> upgrade -> invite friends.
- Drucker lens: one owner metric for MVP: players who complete first training, first trial, and first bracket spectate.
- Analyst lens: biggest risk is unclear agency. Players must feel their training choices affected the champion.

## Recommended MVP

Build **House Trials Arena: RPS + One Active Minigame + Timed Bracket Event**.

MVP includes:
- 4 Houses
- 12 starter Champions
- champion trait customization
- RPS auto-duel mode
- one active training minigame
- timed 8-player bracket
- house points leaderboard
- cosmetic rewards only
- public replay/results wall

Do not include:
- real money
- crypto/SOL
- gambling language
- user-generated freeform prompts for kids
- complex AI explanation text
- private hidden strategy that feels unfair
